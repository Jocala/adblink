Refresh ADB

Queries the adb server for connected devices and updates the Status column to match what adb reports. It also re-reads the saved device list, so newly added or edited devices appear as well.

A device that was reconnected outside adblink (or dropped without adblink noticing) shows its true state after a refresh.

Refresh ADB does not connect devices on its own. To reconnect a network device after a reboot, select it and use Connect (or double-click its row).

If you have questions or comments, please email jocala@jocala.com. adblink produces a log file, adblink.log, with each run. Include it in your email or post if you're having problems.
