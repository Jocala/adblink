This dialog allows you to start or stop an Android application. You must know the "package name" of the application you want to start/stop, i.e. org.xbmc.kodi for Kodi. One suggested use is to stop Kodi from running when you wish to do a restore, then restarting it after the restore is done.

If you have questions or comments, please email jocala@jocala.com. adblink produces a log file, adblink.log, with each run. Include it in your email or post if you're having problems.
